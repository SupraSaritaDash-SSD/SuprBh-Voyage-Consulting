Scaled Agile Scrum Master
Program Overview

A practical, enterprise-focused workshop designed to help Scrum Masters, Team Leads, Agile Practitioners, and Delivery Professionals understand how Agile teams operate successfully within a Scaled Agile environment.

The program combines Agile principles, Scrum practices, SAFe concepts, facilitation techniques, team coaching approaches, and real-world enterprise examples to help participants support value delivery at scale.

Target Audience

Scrum Masters
Team Leads
Agile Coaches
Delivery Managers
Project Managers
Product Owners
Aspiring Release Train Engineers (RTEs)

Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

Understand Agile values, principles, and Lean thinking
Apply Scrum effectively within enterprise environments
Understand Agile at Scale concepts
Explain the purpose of Agile Release Trains (ARTs)
Participate effectively in PI Planning
Identify and manage dependencies across teams
Facilitate continuous improvement initiatives
Support organizational agility and transformation efforts
Enable high-performing Agile teams
