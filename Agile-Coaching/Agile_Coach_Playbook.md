Agile Coach Playbook v1.0
Purpose

The Agile Coach Playbook provides a structured approach to enabling organizational agility through coaching, mentoring, facilitation, training, and leadership engagement.

The goal of Agile Coaching is not merely Agile adoption but sustainable business agility that improves customer outcomes, delivery effectiveness, employee engagement, and organizational performance.
Agile Coach Mission

An Agile Coach helps organizations:

Deliver value faster
Improve team effectiveness
Build Agile leadership capability
Enable continuous improvement
Foster a culture of learning and adaptability

Common Agile Coach Anti-Patterns
Teaching Instead of Coaching

The goal is capability building, not dependency.

Agile by Process

Focus on outcomes, not ceremonies.

Ignoring Leadership

Transformation cannot succeed without leadership engagement.

Local Optimization

Improve the entire system, not individual teams alone.

Agile Coach Principles
Coach the system, not just the team.

Lead through influence, not authority.
