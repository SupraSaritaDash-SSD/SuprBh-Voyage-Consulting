PI Planning Playbook
Purpose

Program Increment (PI) Planning is a cadence-based planning event that aligns Agile teams, business stakeholders, Product Management, and leadership around a shared mission and objectives for the upcoming Program Increment.

PI Planning establishes alignment, identifies dependencies, mitigates risks, and enables predictable delivery of business value.

PI Planning Objectives: 
Align teams to strategic goals
Establish common mission and vision
Identify dependencies early
Improve collaboration across teams
Manage risks proactively
Increase delivery predictability

Participants
Business Owners

Provide business context and priorities.

Product Management

Present vision, roadmap, and priorities.

Release Train Engineer (RTE)

Facilitates the event and ensures alignment.

Scrum Masters

Support team planning and dependency management.

Product Owners

Clarify features, priorities, and acceptance criteria.

Agile Teams

Estimate, plan, and commit to objectives.

Common Anti-Patterns
Too Much Work

Teams overcommit.

Missing Stakeholders

Decisions delayed.

Weak Backlog Readiness

Planning becomes estimation sessions.

Hidden Dependencies

Delivery risks emerge later.

Lack of Business Participation

Reduced alignment and ownership.
