Lean Portfolio Management Guide v1.0
Purpose

Lean Portfolio Management (LPM) aligns strategy, funding, governance, and execution to ensure organizations deliver the highest value initiatives while maintaining business agility.

LPM helps bridge the gap between executive strategy and team execution.

Why Lean Portfolio Management?

Traditional portfolio management often results in:

Long approval cycles
Excessive governance
Slow decision-making
Misaligned priorities
Funding delays

Lean Portfolio Management improves responsiveness while maintaining governance.

Lean Portfolio Management Objectives
Align strategy with execution
Accelerate value delivery
Improve investment decisions
Increase organizational agility
Enhance portfolio visibility
