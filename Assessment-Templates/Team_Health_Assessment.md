Team Health Assessment Framework v1.0
Purpose

The Team Health Assessment helps Agile teams evaluate their effectiveness, identify improvement opportunities, and build a culture of continuous improvement.

The assessment should be used as a coaching tool rather than a performance evaluation mechanism.

Assessment Scale
Score	Meaning

2	Disagree
3	Neutral
4	Agree
5	Strongly Agree
