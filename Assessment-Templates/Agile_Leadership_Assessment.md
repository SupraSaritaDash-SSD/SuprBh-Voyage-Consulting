Agile Leadership Assessment Framework v1.0
Purpose

The Agile Leadership Assessment evaluates leadership behaviors, decision-making patterns, organizational influence, and alignment with Agile values and principles.

The goal is to identify leadership strengths, growth opportunities, and organizational enablers required for business agility
