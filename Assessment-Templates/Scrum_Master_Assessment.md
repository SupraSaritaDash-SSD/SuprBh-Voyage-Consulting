Purpose

This assessment helps evaluate the effectiveness, capability, and growth areas of Scrum Masters supporting Agile teams.

The objective is development and coaching, not performance evaluation.

Maturity Levels
Level 1 – Ceremony Facilitator

Focuses mainly on events and reporting.

Level 2 – Team Coordinator

Supports team execution and delivery.

Level 3 – Team Coach

Improves team effectiveness and collaboration.

Level 4 – Agile Leader

Influences stakeholders and drives improvement.

Level 5 – Agile Coach

Coaches teams, leaders, and organizational systems.
