# Coaching Questions Library

## Team Coaching

- What challenge are we trying to solve?
- What assumptions are we making?
- What would success look like?

---

## Scrum Master Coaching

- What pattern do you observe?
- What options have you explored?
- What is preventing progress?

---

## Product Owner Coaching

- What customer problem are we solving?
- How will we measure success?
- Why is this feature important?

---

## Leadership Coaching

- What outcome are we pursuing?
- What behaviors are we encouraging?
- What would happen if we empowered the team?

---

## Transformation Coaching

- What problem are we trying to solve?
- What evidence supports this change?
- How will we know transformation is successful?
